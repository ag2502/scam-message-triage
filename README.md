# Scam-Message Triage

Forward a suspicious message, get back a **risk score**, the **scam type** and
**what to do next**, with a plain-language reason.

Instant-payment systems (Pix in Brazil, UPI in India, SPEI in Mexico, Zelle in
the US, Faster Payments in the UK) settle in seconds and are hard to reverse, so
scammers push victims with urgent WhatsApp/SMS messages: a "relative" on a new
number, a forged payment receipt, a fake bank agent. This project gives people a
quick check *before* they pay.

The engine is rail-agnostic and language-pluggable. English is the first locale;
PT-BR and ES are next.
