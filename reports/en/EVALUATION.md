# Evaluation: EN triage model v0.3.0

_Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py` on 2026-10-03._ Trained on 9946 messages (synthetic, uci_sms_ham).

**How to read this.** Thresholds are fixed at training time, from template-grouped 5-fold out-of-fold scores on train+val, at a target
false-positive rate (FPR), and then applied unchanged to each evaluation set. The FPR column is what a deployment
would see, not a number tuned on the evaluation data. `model_high` / `model_medium` are the thresholds the bot uses
for its *high* / *medium* risk levels (target 1% / 5% FPR).
Precision is shown on the evaluation set as-is and re-weighted to assumed scam prevalences, since the share of scams
among messages people forward to a checker is unknown.

## Caveats

- The training data is synthetic. Held-out-template scores measure generalization to new *phrasings* of known scam
  patterns, not to new scam *types*. The blind challenge set is the better (but small) signal.
- Conversation-starter scams ("sorry, wrong number… where are you from?") carry no scam content in the first
  message; single-message triage cannot reliably catch them.
- Real legit negatives come from the UCI SMS corpus (UK/Singapore SMS, 2000s), whose style differs from modern
  WhatsApp chat. FPR on today's messages may differ.
- Next: collect consented, labelled forwarded messages from the bot to replace synthetic test data.
